Step-by-step: logging a card correctly

  1. Pick the game first. This decides which free database (Scryfall / pokemontcg.io / YGOPRODeck) gets searched — picking the wrong game means the lookup will never find a match.
  2. Photograph the front of the card flat, in good light, filling the frame. Glare, blur, or a steep angle is the most common reason OCR guesses badly. If the first attempt reads poorly, use Retake photo rather than pushing ahead with a bad guess.
  3. Read the suggested matches, don't just tap the first one. OCR gives a starting guess, not a fact — check the set name and rarity shown against your physical card before selecting. If nothing matches, use None of these — enter manually and type the name yourself.
  4. Print type is always your call. Foil, holo, and reverse-holo can catch light differently in every photo, so the app will never guess this for you. Picking wrong here is the single most common way collection value gets inflated — take the extra second to check the physical card.
  5. Condition is a self-assessed estimate, not a grade. Compare your card against the tiers in the condition dropdown (same scale as docs/GRADING_REFERENCE.md). When in doubt, pick the lower tier — it's easy to revise a value up later, harder to trust a collection that's optimistically graded.
  6. Check comps before typing a price. Tap the TCGplayer / eBay / StarCityGames links — they open pre-filled searches for the exact name you entered. Use eBay's sold listings filter (already applied) over active listings, since active listings are asking prices, not what anything actually sold for.
  7. Confirm & add is the only thing that logs the card. Every step before this can be backed out of freely — nothing is saved until you tap that button.

Frequently asked

Where is my data actually stored?
In this browser's local storage, on this device, only. There's no server and no account — nobody else can see it, and it won't appear on another device unless you export and import the CSV yourself.

If I send someone this app's link, do they see my cards?
No. Both the Collection and For-Sale Archive tabs start empty for every new visitor — each person's data lives only in their own browser. Sharing the link shares the tool, not your inventory.

Why won't it just tell me if a card is foil?
Foil, holo, and reverse-holo effects depend on lighting and angle in a way a flat photo can't reliably capture — and foil/normal prices differ substantially. Guessing wrong here would silently inflate or deflate your collection value, so it's always a manual choice.

OCR keeps missing / lookup found nothing — what do I do?
Retake the photo in better light, or just type the name into the search box yourself and tap "Search this name." You can also skip lookup entirely with "None of these — enter manually" and fill in the set/rarity by hand.

Why is pricing just links instead of an automatic number?
Automatic pricing would mean either paying for an API or scraping marketplaces in a way that breaks quietly and can't be trusted. Link-outs stay free, stay accurate to what you actually see, and never auto-refresh a number behind your back.

How do I move my collection to a new phone?
Collection tab → Export CSV, then on the new device open the app and use Import CSV. Same pattern for the For-Sale Archive tab.
