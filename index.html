How this works

This is a static, single-file web app — no server, no database, no account, no cost. It installs to your phone's home screen via Safari's Add to Home Screen and runs entirely in your browser. Your collection is stored in this browser's local storage; export to CSV to back it up or move it to another device.

Card identification is never automatic. A photo is run through on-device OCR (Tesseract.js) and matched against free public card databases, but every card requires your explicit confirmation — including print type — before it's added. This is intentional: a misidentified card inflates collection value and breaks the point of tracking it.

Pricing is link-out only: the app opens pre-filled searches on TCGplayer, eBay sold listings, and StarCityGames for you to review and enter a value yourself. Nothing is auto-fetched or auto-refreshed.

See docs/GRADING_REFERENCE.md and docs/SCHEMA_REFERENCE.md in the repo for the full condition scale and CSV column definitions.

Developer tools — self-tests
